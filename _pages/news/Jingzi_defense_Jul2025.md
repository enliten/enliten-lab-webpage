# Congratulations to Dr. Jingzi Liu on Her Successful Ph.D. Defense!
{:.no_toc}

On **July 11**, Jingzi Liu successfully defended her Ph.D. dissertation titled *“Data-driven Solutions for Power Systems During Energy Transition.”* Her research focused on leveraging data-driven methods to address challenges in power systems as they undergo rapid transformation toward sustainability. Congratulations to **Dr. Jingzi Liu** on reaching this significant academic milestone!

Dr. Jingzi’s dissertation made impactful contributions across four major areas:  
- **Machine learning (ML) online applications**: Proposed three data-driven methods to correct defective input features and enable robust ML in power systems.  
- **Demand response (DR) assessment**: Developed an incentive-based DR capacity assessment tool to improve energy flexibility and sustainability.  
- **Affordable energy market design**: Designed an innovative peer-to-peer (P2P) energy trading fusion market emphasizing energy justice and decentralization.  
- **Grid benefit assessment**: Created a grid-level benefit assessment tool to support the deployment of air-source heat pumps integrated with thermal energy storage.

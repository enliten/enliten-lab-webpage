# Chenchen Li Receives the EECS Outstanding Graduate Research Assistants Award
{:.no_toc}

**Chenchen Li received the EECS Outstanding Graduate Research Assistants Award**

Students, faculty, and alumni from the Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science were recently honored at the Gonzalez Awards Banquet, held April 30. Chenchen Li received the EECS Outstanding Graduate Research Assistants Award - Electrical Engineering.

Congratulations to Chenchen Li!

For more details, visit the full article [here](https://eecs.utk.edu/2026-gonzalez-awards/).

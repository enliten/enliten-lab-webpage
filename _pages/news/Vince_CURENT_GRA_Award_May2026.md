# Vince Wilson Receives the 2026 Handong Gui Outstanding Graduate Research Assistant Award
{:.no_toc}

**Vince Wilson received the 2026 Handong Gui Outstanding Graduate Research Assistant Award in power systems**

Vince Wilson, advised by Dr. Fran Li, received the 2026 Handong Gui Outstanding Graduate Research Assistant Awards in power systems. They were recognized at a luncheon hosted by CURENT faculty on May 13 when other students were recognized for their leadership and support during the [2026 Industry Conference](https://curent.utk.edu/industry-leaders-and-scholars-convene-for-the-2026-curent-industry-conference-at-utk/).

Congratulations to Vince Wilson!

For more details, visit the full article [here](https://curent.utk.edu/2026-handong-gui-outstanding-graduate-research-awards-presented-to-curent-students/).

# Congratulations to Dr. Chenchen Li on Her Successful Ph.D. Defense!
{:.no_toc}

On **July 14th**, Chenchen Li successfully defended her Ph.D. dissertation titled *“**Toward Affordability, Vulnerability, and Economic Efficiency in Power System Planning and Operation**”*. Her research focused on incorporating energy affordability, energy vulnerability into the DG planning decisions, developing a data center planning model from the perspective of private investors, and mitigating circular flows in HVDC-integrated transmission systems. Congratulations to **Dr. Chenchen Li** on this academic achievement!
